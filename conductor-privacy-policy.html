Conductor — Privacy Policy

Conductor is a browser extension that turns web pages into tools and operates them by chat under your control.

What Conductor stores

Your settings, your skills, your activity log, and — if you use the API Vault — API definitions and any API keys you choose to save. All of this is stored only in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device. It is not synced, not backed up by us, and not transmitted to us. We operate no servers and receive none of your data.

What leaves your device

Only what you direct. If you use the local model route (Ollama), page content never leaves your computer. If you choose the cloud route, chat content is sent to OpenRouter using your own API key, subject to OpenRouter's privacy policy. Conductor never sends your saved API Vault keys to any model provider; a vault key is attached only to requests to that key's own origin, executed in your own browser tab.

What we collect

Nothing. No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports, no advertising identifiers.
